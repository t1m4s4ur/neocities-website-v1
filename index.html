general

  • my art is for personal use only and i own all rights to my work. putting my art in any ai engines is prohibited
  • i have every right to decline your commission if i feel that my art skill cannot meet your request or when the commission falls under the subjects i dont do
  • i also can refuse to draw for you depending on my background check result (i dont serve radqueers, z00s, p3d0s and anyone related)
  • i try not to overwork myself and because of that i can occasionally take breaks. some pieces could be finished within a few hours and some can take up to a couple of days depending on the difficulty and my wellbeing
  • if youre going to post my art anywhere please credit me by linking this site or any of my socials
  • to request a commission or ask me about anything commission related you can pm me on any of my listed social media (im most active on tg, bsky and furaffinity)
  • i encourage you to provide brief desc. of your characters, desc. of the setting theyre in and references, poses along with ideas. anything youd want me to add and anything youd want to see in the finished piece
  • i also ask you to have a 2D reference sheet of your character(s). or a 2D drawing with your character(s) since its easier for me to color and draw overall if its not 3D
  • if youre ordering a custom character design please provide a color scheme (e.g. a palette/description), species and any details youd want to add. having a moodboard for the character would also be great
  • prices either can go up or drop depending on demand/economy/urgency/other reasons (that will be listed here). prices can also drop for a limited amount of time or slots
  • additional stuff (like backgrounds and such) could be added later! trying to figure out how to make it look pretty lol

payment

  • i take 100% payment upfront
  • at the moment my comms are non-refundable (if youre paying in anything except rubles).
